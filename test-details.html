Urine Routine Test

Purpose

The urine routine test is used to examine urine and identify possible abnormalities.

Sample Required

A fresh urine sample collected in a clean sample container.

Preparation Instructions

Follow the instructions provided by the laboratory staff.
Provide the required sample in the prescribed container.

Normal Reference Range

Reference values may vary depending on the parameter and laboratory method used.

Report Delivery Time

Reports are generally available within 24 hours.

Important Instructions

Important: Please provide a properly collected sample.

Follow all instructions given by the laboratory staff.

Fasting requirements depend on the specific test.

Please contact the laboratory for test-specific preparation instructions.
